Crypto Trading & Analysis Masterclass for Beginners

New to crypto trading? Follow this automated blueprint covering fund management, fundamental & technical analysis, and strategic entry-exit execution.

1. Capital & Risk Management

Never invest all your funds into a single asset. Split your total portfolio into 3-4 parts. Risk only 2-5% of your total capital per trade to safeguard against severe market corrections.

2. Fundamental & Technical Analysis

Evaluate project utility, team credibility, and circulating supply limits (Fundamental). Use support/resistance levels and indicators like RSI on charts to time your entries (Technical).

3. When to BUY & SELL

Buy: During high-fear market dips and strong support zones using Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA).
Sell: Take partial profits at resistance targets instead of waiting for absolute peaks.

4. Spot Trading Best Practices

Stick strictly to secure Spot Trading as a beginner. Avoid high-risk leverage or futures trading until you master market volatility and emotion control.

Crypto Anti-Scam & Security Guidelines

Staying safe in the cryptocurrency world is your number one priority. Follow these essential safety guidelines to protect your funds from fraudsters:

1. Never Share Private Keys

Your 12 or 24-word recovery phrase gives full control of your wallet. No official platform or admin will ever ask for them.

2. Beware of Phishing Links

Always check URLs before connecting Web3 wallets. Scammers create duplicate domains mimicking popular platforms.

3. Avoid Fake High-Yield Offers

Be skeptical of double-your-crypto scams, guaranteed daily returns, or random airdrop DMs promising free fortunes.

4. Use Secure Wallets

Store significant long-term crypto assets in secure hardware wallets rather than leaving funds on exchange hot wallets.
